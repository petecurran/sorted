## Names

Mersey Vale City Council is fictional, and its logo was made for this project. Real bodies named in `content/whose_job.json` and `seed/lads_lcr.geojson` (Network Rail, National Highways, and the neighbouring Halton, Knowsley, Sefton, St Helens and Wirral councils) appear only as the organisations responsible for their land. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
