Is it fly-tipping? Say "no" if there is no dumped waste in the photo (a clean street, people, pets, food, a room indoors, a screen or a document), or if the waste has plainly been put out properly: wheelie bins, a skip, a building site, or bags set out neatly beside bins. Say "unsure" only if you really cannot tell.

Size, using the Environment Agency bands. Judge the whole pile:
- "Single black bag": one bag and nothing else.
- "Single item": one item on its own, such as a mattress, a sofa, a fridge or a shopping trolley.
- "Car boot or less": up to about 1 cubic metre, what fits in a car boot: up to 5 bags, or one piece of furniture with a few bags.
- "Small van load": about 1 to 4 cubic metres: 6 to 15 bags, furniture with several bags, or a heap up to waist height.
- "Transit van load": about 4 to 10 cubic metres: 16 to 30 bags, or a heap taller than a person.
- "Tipper lorry load" or "Significant/multiple loads": much more than that.

Waste type is what makes up most of the pile. Use "Black bags - household" only when bags are most of it. Furniture, mattresses and carpets are "Other household waste". Garden cuttings and hedge trimmings are "Green". Rubble, plasterboard, tiles and bags of plaster are "Construction/demolition/excavation". Fridges and freezers are "White goods". Fuel cans and oil drums are "Chemical drums, oil or fuel".

Decision: "specialist" if the hazards could include asbestos, clinical waste, chemicals, paint or fuel. "hold_for_officer" if it is mostly household black bags, or trade waste that could contain documents. "not_a_fly_tip" if fly_tip is "no". Otherwise "clear_now".
